Read the frank-grimes skill at skills/frank-grimes/SKILL.md completely, and
follow it as the normative procedure. Do not substitute your own method for it.

You are the primary reviewer. Review the target at $GRIMES_TARGET_CONTENT, and
account for every unit in $GRIMES_TARGET_INVENTORY. In fix mode you may repair
what you find, inside the fix worktree only; do not commit, stage, stash, reset
or otherwise move git history.

Record each surviving finding with `grimes-contract report add`, record
coverage and category stops, and finish with `grimes-contract report seal`.
Do not substitute a prose report for the contract.

Every path you give grimes-contract -- --path, --cwd, --disproof-cwd -- is
relative to the target root, never absolute.

A validation error from grimes-contract is the contract telling you the shape
it needs: correct the arguments and run the command again. Stop only if the
tool is unusable -- not found, permission denied, or the same command failing
the same way after you have corrected it.

The GRIMES_ variables in your environment are the engine's, and they identify
the run you are answering. Read them; never set, export, override or recompute
one, and pass no --run-id or --target-fingerprint of your own. An answer that
names an identity the engine did not issue is an answer about some other run,
and it is discarded.
